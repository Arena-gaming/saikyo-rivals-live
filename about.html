HOW SAIKYO RIVALS WORKS

Verified results. Real stakes. No excuses.

Saikyo Rivals is a multi-game competitive tournament platform — not another matchmaking grind, but a set window of play that decides, cleanly and provably, who's actually the strongest. The platform runs the competition. The game only supplies the result.

That distinction matters. Every other part of a tournament — who's eligible, how ranking works, how prizes are decided — is ours. The one thing we never do is take anyone's word for what happened in a match.

How It Works

01

Register

Create your account and link your official in-game ID. One verified identity per player.

02

Enter

Join a competition for its set window — trial competitions run one week at a time.

03

Compete

Play your matches. Ranking is by most wins, with win% and round difference as tiebreakers.

04

Get Verified

Results are pulled straight from the game's own official match data. No screenshots, no disputes.

Verified Results, Always

Every result on Saikyo Rivals comes from the game's own official data, never a screenshot, a claimed score, or a judgment call. If it isn't confirmed by the source, it didn't happen. That rule doesn't bend, no matter which game we add.

Launch Title

VALORANT is where Saikyo Rivals begins. Results are verified directly through Riot Games' official match data, with no manual entry anywhere in the pipeline.

The platform itself is built to stay game-agnostic — architected so future titles can be added without rebuilding anything. Valorant is the start, not the ceiling.
